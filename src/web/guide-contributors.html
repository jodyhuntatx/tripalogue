Sharing your trip photos and videos

You've been invited to add your photos and videos to a travelogue. TripAlogue puts each one in the right part of the trip and on the map using the time and place your phone or camera recorded, so it needs the originals, not copies.

Do
  • Upload straight from your phone's photo library or from your computer.
  • Keep location on when you share or export.
  • Upload videos too: they are kept at full quality.
Don't
  • Forward photos through WhatsApp, Messenger, Signal or Telegram first: chat apps shrink them and remove the time and place.
  • Send screenshots or "small size" exports.

1. Open your invitation

  1. Open the invitation link from your email and sign in with that email address. The sign-in link arrives by email; there is no password.
  2. You'll land in the travelogue. Open the Upload tab (in the Play view, beside Items).

2. Choose your originals

iPhone or iPad. In the upload window, choose Photo Library and pick your photos. If you share from the Photos app instead, tap Options at the top of the share sheet and turn on Location and All Photos Data.

Android. Pick photos from your gallery or file manager. If your gallery app has a setting that removes location when sharing, turn it off while you add your trip photos.

Mac. In Photos, select the photos and choose File › Export › Export Unmodified Original, then upload the exported files. Files copied straight from a camera's memory card are originals too.

Windows. Upload the files from the folder your phone or camera imported them into.

Big uploads can take a while. If the connection drops or you close the page, choose the same files again and the upload continues where it stopped. Files you already sent are recognised and skipped.

3. Using a camera?

  • Cameras don't set their own clocks. If the camera's clock was wrong (or still on home time), that's fine: the trip's editor can move all of that camera's photos at once. Just tell them roughly how far off it was.
  • Most cameras record no location. Photos without one still go into the right part of the trip by their time.

Why originals matter

Every original carries a capture time and, from a phone, a location. The travelogue uses these to place your photos in the right chapter and on the map, with no sorting by hand. Copies made by chat apps lose both, and then someone has to place each one by hand.
