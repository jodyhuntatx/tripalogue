Sending your trip photos and videos

A fellow traveler is making a travelogue of the trip and would like your photos and videos. It puts each one in the right part of the trip and on the map using the time and place your phone or camera recorded, so it needs the originals, not copies.

Do
  • Send straight from your phone's photo library or your computer: AirDrop, a shared folder (iCloud, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive), a file-transfer service, or a USB stick.
  • Keep location on when you share or export.
  • Send videos too, at full quality.
Don't
  • Send photos through WhatsApp, Messenger, Signal or Telegram: chat apps shrink them and remove the time and place.
  • Send screenshots or "small size" exports.

Getting the originals out

iPhone or iPad. In Photos, select the photos, tap Share, then tap Options at the top of the share sheet and turn on Location and All Photos Data. AirDrop to a Mac keeps the originals.

Android. Share from your gallery or file manager to a shared folder or a file-transfer service. If your gallery app has a setting that removes location when sharing, turn it off while you send your trip photos.

Mac. In Photos, select the photos and choose File › Export › Export Unmodified Original, then send the exported files. Files copied straight from a camera's memory card are originals too.

Windows. Send the files from the folder your phone or camera imported them into.

Sending everything again is fine: files the travelogue already has are recognised and skipped.

Using a camera?

  • Cameras don't set their own clocks. If the camera's clock was wrong (or still on home time), that's fine: all of that camera's photos can be moved at once. Just say roughly how far off it was.
  • Most cameras record no location. Photos without one still go into the right part of the trip by their time.

Why originals matter

Every original carries a capture time and, from a phone, a location. The travelogue uses these to place your photos in the right chapter and on the map, with no sorting by hand. Copies made by chat apps lose both, and then each one has to be placed by hand.
